## 🚀 What It Is
Fontself Maker for Windows is an add-in that plugs into Adobe Illustrator and Adobe Photoshop, turning the vectors or brushstrokes you already draw into installable fonts. It runs as a panel inside your Adobe app, so you can design a typeface, script, or icon set and export it as OpenType without exporting to a separate program. Whether you work in vectors or in raster, Fontself Maker keeps the whole "letter to font" process in one place.

> Draw your letters where you already work, drop them into the panel, and walk away with a real font.

## ✨ Key Features
- 🎯 **One-panel workflow** — the extension opens right inside Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop, no separate font app required.
- ✏️ **Vector and raster support** — turn crisp Illustrator paths or textured Photoshop artwork into glyphs.
- 💾 **OpenType export** — generate a standard .otf font that installs on Windows and any app that reads system fonts.
- 📐 **Precise spacing** — control side bearings, kerning, and ligatures so your font sets cleanly.
- 🌈 **Color fonts** — assemble layered color and SVG typefaces for playful, multi-color lettering.

## 📥 Setup
1. Press the **Download** button above to grab the latest Fontself Maker package.
2. Install the add-in — run the installer or add it through your Adobe extension manager — and follow the prompts.
3. Start Adobe Illustrator or Photoshop, open the Fontself Maker panel from the Window menu, and activate it with your license or the free trial.

## 🎯 First Steps
1. Open your lettering project in Adobe Illustrator or Photoshop.
2. Reveal the Fontself Maker panel via the Window menu.
3. Highlight your letters and drag them into the panel so they map to the right characters.
4. Tune spacing and kerning until your text sets nicely.
5. Save and install your new OpenType font on Windows.

## ❓ Questions
**Is Fontself Maker really free?**
Fontself Maker is a paid add-in, and people searching "fontself maker for illustrator cc free download" or "fontself maker illustrator free" are pointed to the free trial/demo. The trial lets you explore font-building inside Illustrator before you buy — it is not a free full copy.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
It runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11, provided you have a compatible, recent version of Adobe Illustrator or Photoshop to host the add-in.

**Is my work kept private?**
Yes. Your artwork stays inside your Adobe app on your own machine, and exported fonts are written locally to your Windows drive.
